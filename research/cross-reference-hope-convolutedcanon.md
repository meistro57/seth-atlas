### 1. Model A and Model C are NOT mutually exclusive
A shared experience-generating process (A) and a shared cultural interpretation process (C)
can operate together: sleep paralysis may generate unusual perceptions while cultural knowledge
shapes how they are subsequently interpreted. The Mayer & Fuhrmann study measures
retrospectively reported interpretation differences; it does NOT establish that the original
experience remained unchanged.
